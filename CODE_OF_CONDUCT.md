# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Huginn to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

## Standards

Encouraged: kindness, constructive feedback, respect for different viewpoints, accepting
criticism gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Not tolerated: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing other people's
private information, and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/silencelen/huginn/security/advisories/new), or with
GitHub's report-abuse feature on the comment or user concerned. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a participant.
