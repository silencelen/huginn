Mark

The raven stands upright, facing right: twelve straight edges, a round eye cut as a hole, and one hairline fold along the folded wing. It is one evenodd path in a 108 box, drawn on a 24 by 16 lattice so the terminal renderings are exact rasters of it.

lockup.svg and lockup-dark.svg · lowercase huginn in Plex Mono SemiBold outlines at −6 %, the raven standing where the cursor would be · wordmark.svg without the raven
Sizes64 px and up: the mark with the fold. 32 px and below: the solid, since the fold antialiases away. 16 to 22 px on a bone tile: the head drawing. The eye survives every size.
ColourInk on bone, bone on the ground, or one state tint. Never outlined, never a gradient, shadow or second colour.
PlacementUpright and facing right, never mirrored. The launcher scales the bird 0.72 about (52,43) so its farthest vertex stays inside the 33 dp safe ring. The raven sits inside type only in the cursor lockup.

Tile

The small form for the tray, the notification icon and favicons: the raven cut out of a rounded square (64 box, corner radius 14) at 0.84 of the mark, the eye a 4 by 4 cell on whole pixels so it is exactly one pixel at 16. The tile takes the state colour; needs-you adds the badge (error, r 9 at 53,11).

waiting · working · needs you, at 48, 24 and 16 · the tray keeps these dark-scheme tints on every taskbar · TrayIcons.kt draws the tile at 64 and lets the OS downscale

States

Seven words, the same everywhere: the apps, the tray tooltip, the terminal title, the CLI and Telegram. The apps draw dots; terminals print the glyph, or the 7-bit mark where output can reach Windows PowerShell 5.1. Needs-you is the accent lit up, never the error red: a question is not a failure.

Dots8 dp for live states. 6 dp for waiting, offline and settled things. One dot per row, in the state's colour, nothing else.
The accentPrimary means live and you: the working dot and pulse, the selected item, links, the cursor, and the one filled button on a screen, which is Send. Never a section label, a warning, or "online".
RedError is for failures and destructive verbs. Stop and Interrupt are plain outlined buttons; a soft verb is onSurfaceVariant; a destructive verb and its confirm button are error.

Severity

Every meter and threshold reads one ramp: normal is the outline, warning is the needs-you step, critical is the error red. Warning at 70 %, critical at 90 %, unless the daemon sends its own word.

IBM Plex Mono leads: the wordmark, the display role, labels, terminal, code and key-value rows. IBM Plex Sans reads: titles and body, so transcripts stay comfortable. Both are bundled in the apps (Mono 400, 500, 600 and italic 400; Sans 400, 500, 600).

TrackingNegative only above 22. The mono is never letter-spaced, except labelMedium's +6 %. The wordmark is −6 %.
Where the mono goesSession names, labels, timestamps, code and key-value rows. Phone and desktop draw the same bundled glyphs; the terminal view draws cell by cell, so it never forms ligatures.
Headers and taglineSection headers in Title Case, no uppercase tracking. The tagline is Plex Mono italic, one line, never wrapped.

Motion

Motion is a slow pulse, not a spinner: it says something is thinking, not that a network is busy. Three pieces move in the apps, and nothing else does.

The stepped pulse
PulseDot · ten steps of 160 ms, alpha 0.35 → 1 → 0.35 over 1.6 s, in primary · the live marker in the work strip, the session header and the overview; a SettledDot in outline when nothing runs · it ticks only while frames are drawn
thinking
The thinking line
ThinkingLine in chat · the word in onSurfaceVariant, alpha 0.35 ↔ 1, 900 ms each way, between a run starting and its first token
The side panel
Desktop shell · the panel opens and closes over 150 ms
RestraintNothing loops at full energy, nothing overshoots, and the resting state is a still dot. A new animation earns its place against these three.

Voice

The nameHuginn in prose; huginn as the command and the wordmark. Never HUGINN.
State wordsworking · needs you · waiting · shell · ok · error · info. "Needs you" is a question, never a failure; a failure is an error.
QuietNo emoji anywhere. Lowercase output; nothing loud but needs you. The raven carries the character; the type and the colour carry the quality.
